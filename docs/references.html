References#

Mosher#

Lee Mosher. A user’s guide to the mapping class group: once punctured surfaces.

Massuyeau#

Gwénaël Massuyeau. Lectures on mapping class groups, braid groups and formality.

Tutte (1963)#

W. T. Tutte. How to Draw a Graph. Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society, s3-13(1):743-767, 1963.

Establishes that a triconnected planar graph, drawn with its outer face fixed as a convex polygon and every other vertex placed at a positive weighted average of its neighbors, always yields a planar straight-line drawing with every face convex. Used throughout combisurf.layout.tutte_barycentric as the correctness argument behind tutte_layout(), and throughout combisurf.layout.gluing as the guarantee that keeps a composed piece’s entire drawing within the convex hull of its own fixed boundary.

Hopcroft & Kahn (1992)#

John E. Hopcroft and Peter C. Kahn. A Paradigm for Robust Geometric Algorithms. Algorithmica, 7(1-6):339-380, 1992.

Cited in combisurf.layout.tutte_barycentric for the generalization of Tutte’s theorem to asymmetric edge weights (the two directions of an edge may carry different weights, so long as both are positive).

Eades (1992)#

Peter Eades. Drawing Free Trees. Bulletin of the Institute for Combinatorics and its Applications, 5:10-36, 1992.

Source of the radial tree-drawing algorithm (DrawSubTree1) implemented as eades_radial_layout().

Eades & Garvan (1996)#

Peter Eades and Patrick Garvan. Drawing Stressed Planar Graphs in Three Dimensions. In Graph Drawing (GD 1995), Lecture Notes in Computer Science 1027, pages 212-223. Springer, 1996.

Cited in combisurf.layout.tutte_barycentric for the observation that plain uniform-weight Tutte embeddings can have an edge-length ratio growing exponentially in the number of vertices, motivating the alternative weighting schemes implemented there.

Floater (2003)#

Michael S. Floater. Mean Value Coordinates. Computer Aided Geometric Design, 20(1):19-27, 2003.

Source of the mean value coordinate weights implemented as mean_value_weights().

Chiu, Eppstein & Goodrich (2023)#

Man-Kwun Chiu, David Eppstein, and Michael T. Goodrich. Manipulating Weights to Improve Stress-Graph Drawings of 3-Connected Planar Graphs. In Graph Drawing and Network Visualization (GD 2023). arXiv:2307.10527.

Source of the edge-length-ratio drawing-quality metric (edge_length_ratio()) and the BFS-depth weighting scheme (bfs_depth_weights(), bfs_depth_layout()).
